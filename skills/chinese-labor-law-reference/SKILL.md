# Chinese Labor Law Reference

## Use When

- The brief involves an employee dispute (resignation, termination, demotion, transfer, pay cut, bonus, severance)
- The brief involves a workplace injury or occupational disease (工伤认定 / 职业病诊断 / 劳动能力鉴定)
- The brief involves working hours, overtime, rest days, or paid leave
- The brief involves a female employee in any of the three protected periods (孕期 / 产期 / 哺乳期)
- The brief involves wage payment, deductions, minimum wage, or wage payment timing
- The brief involves union representation, collective bargaining, or collective contracts
- The brief involves the time-limit for filing labor arbitration or labor litigation
- The brief involves comparing how a labor dispute would play out in a different region

## Reading Rules

- All statute citations follow Hard Rule 1 format: 《劳动合同法》第N条第N款第N项. Always include 条 / 款 / 项 to the level the brief requires.
- Always confirm the statute version date. Labor Contract Law: 2007 enacted, 2012 amendment (articles 11, 24, 26, 28, 92, 96 amended). Work-Injury Insurance Regulations: 2003 enacted, 2010 amendment (significantly raised compensation levels). Social Insurance Law: 2010 enacted.
- Regional differences are material — never give a single nationwide answer to a labor dispute question. State the operator's region (your region (configured in personal-company-profile) Province) explicitly and note where the answer would change in a neighboring jurisdiction.
- The 1-year arbitration limitation runs from "the date when the party knew or should have known its rights were infringed" — but for ongoing labor relations (in-employment disputes), the 1-year clock does NOT start until the employment relationship ends. This is a frequent source of error.
- Work-injury identification (工伤认定) has a 1-year application window for the employee (or 30 days for the employer) — missing this window forfeits the work-injury benefit entirely. Always check this clock at the start of any work-injury brief.
- Female-employee 三期 protection is partially preempted by regional implementation rules — Jiangsu's 产假 length differs from Guangdong's. Cite the Jiangsu rule when the operator is the employer; cite the employee's residency-region rule when the operator is the employee (rare in Scenario 1).
- 经济补偿 (statutory severance, Article 47) and 赔偿金 (illegal-termination 2× severance, Article 87) are NOT interchangeable. Confusing them is one of the highest-frequency errors in employer-side analyses.
